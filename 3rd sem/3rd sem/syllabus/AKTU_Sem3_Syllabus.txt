AKTU B.TECH 2ND YEAR - SEMESTER III SYLLABUS
(CSE / CSE-DS and allied branches | Effective Session 2023-24)
│
├───BOE3** / BAS303 — Science Based Open Elective / BSC (Maths-III/IV/V)
│   │   Credits: 4 | L-T-P: 3-1-0
│   │   (Open Elective option: BOE304 — Energy Science and Engineering)
│   │
│   ├───Unit I — Energy and its Usage
│   │       ├── Units and scales of energy use, mechanical energy and transport
│   │       ├── Heat energy: conversion between heat and mechanical energy, entropy, thermodynamic cycles (Carnot, Stirling)
│   │       └── Electromagnetic energy storage/transmission, chemical energy, refrigeration, IC engines, power plant physics, solid-state phenomena
│   │
│   ├───Unit II — Nuclear Energy
│   │       ├── Fundamental forces, quantum mechanics relevant to nuclear physics
│   │       ├── Nuclear forces, binding energy, nuclear reactions, fusion & fission
│   │       └── Reactor physics, design considerations, safety protocols, fuel cycle management
│   │
│   ├───Unit III — Solar Energy
│   │       ├── Introduction to solar energy, fundamentals of solar radiation
│   │       ├── Semiconductor physics, carrier transport mechanisms, p-n junctions
│   │       └── Photovoltaic device characteristics, 1st–3rd generation solar cell technologies
│   │
│   ├───Unit IV — Conventional & Non-Conventional Energy Sources
│   │       ├── Biological energy sources and fossil fuels
│   │       ├── Fluid dynamics and power in wind, wind turbine dynamics
│   │       └── Geothermal energy, ocean thermal conversion, tidal/wave/hydro power
│   │
│   └───Unit V — Systems and Synthesis
│           ├── World energy scenarios, nuclear waste management, climate change impacts
│           ├── Energy storage and conservation, green building concepts, LEED ratings
│           └── Embodied energy analysis, facility energy auditing
│
├───BVE301 / BAS301 — Universal Human Values & Professional Ethics / Technical Communication
│   │   Credits: 3 | L-T-P: 2-1-0
│   │
│   ├───Unit I — Fundamentals of Communication and Voice Dynamics
│   │       ├── Role and purpose of communication, types and flow, barriers to effective communication
│   │       ├── 7 C's of Communication, code and content, stimulus and response theory
│   │       └── Vowel and consonant sound production, tone variation (rising and falling)
│   │
│   ├───Unit II — Communication Skills for Career Building
│   │       ├── CV and résumé preparation, interview techniques, group discussion
│   │       ├── Effective professional profiling, relationship building and networking
│   │       └── Statement of Purpose (SOP), seminar/conference paper writing, technical lecture writing & presentation
│   │
│   ├───Unit III — Communication Skills for Presentation
│   │       ├── Thesis and project report writing, technical proposal development
│   │       └── Idea pitching (process, preparation, structure), speech delivery (passion, poise, illustrative techniques)
│   │
│   ├───Unit IV — Communication and Leadership Development
│   │       ├── Leadership communication strategies, social competence, interpersonal dynamics
│   │       └── Giving/receiving feedback, emotional intelligence, critical thinking enhancement
│   │
│   └───Unit V — Digital Communication and Personality Development
│           ├── Social media content creation for various platforms, ethical digital communication
│           └── Personality assessment frameworks (Maslow, Freud, Vivekananda, Jung typology)
│
├───BCS301 — Data Structure
│   │   Credits: 4 | L-T-P: 3-1-0
│   │
│   ├───Unit I — Introduction, Arrays, Linked Lists
│   │       ├── Basic terminology, ADT, algorithm efficiency, Big-O/Theta/Omega
│   │       ├── Arrays: single/multi-dim, row/column major, sparse matrices
│   │       └── Linked Lists: singly/doubly/circular, polynomial representation
│   │
│   ├───Unit II — Stacks & Queues
│   │       ├── Stack ADT, push/pop, prefix/postfix evaluation
│   │       ├── Recursion: principles, tail recursion, removal of recursion
│   │       └── Queues: circular, dequeue, priority queue
│   │
│   ├───Unit III — Searching & Sorting
│   │       ├── Sequential, index sequential, binary search
│   │       ├── Hashing & collision resolution
│   │       └── Insertion, Selection, Bubble, Quick, Merge, Heap, Radix sort
│   │
│   ├───Unit IV — Trees
│   │       ├── Binary tree, BST, traversals (in/pre/post order)
│   │       ├── Threaded binary trees, Huffman coding
│   │       └── AVL Tree, B Tree, Binary Heaps
│   │
│   └───Unit V — Graphs
│           ├── Representations: adjacency matrix/list
│           ├── DFS, BFS, spanning trees (Prim's, Kruskal's)
│           └── Shortest path: Warshall, Dijkstra
│
├───BCS302 — Computer Organization and Architecture
│   │   Credits: 4 | L-T-P: 3-1-0
│   │
│   ├───Unit I — Functional units, buses, register/bus/memory transfer, addressing modes
│   ├───Unit II — ALU: adders, Booth's algorithm, floating point arithmetic
│   ├───Unit III — Control Unit: instruction cycle, pipelining, micro-programming
│   ├───Unit IV — Memory: RAM/ROM, cache, virtual memory, auxiliary memory
│   └───Unit V — I/O: interfaces, interrupts, DMA, serial communication
│
├───BCS303 — Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic
│   │   Credits: 3 | L-T-P: 2-1-0
│   │
│   ├───Unit I — Set Theory, Relations, POSET & Lattices
│   ├───Unit II — Functions, Boolean Algebra, Karnaugh maps
│   ├───Unit III — Propositional & Predicate Logic
│   ├───Unit IV — Algebraic Structures: Groups, Rings, Fields
│   └───Unit V — Graphs, Combinatorics, Pigeonhole Principle
